📱 About the Application

Family Tree App is an easy-to-use mobile utility designed to help families document, organize, and explore their lineage and family relationships. Whether you are recording historical family records or managing modern family connections, the app provides a clean and reliable experience.

🌐 Bilingual Layout Support

Full support for both English and Urdu scripts so you can record names and family trees naturally.

🔒 Role-Based Access

Dedicated Owner and Admin controls to ensure only authorized family members can edit records.

💾 Backup & Restore

Built-in data backup and restore history to keep your valuable genealogical records safe.
